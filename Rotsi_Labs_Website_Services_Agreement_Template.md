# Website Design, Development and Maintenance Services Agreement

## 1. Source Check

**Date checked:** 17 May 2026  
**Assumed jurisdiction:** Kenya, unless the parties choose another governing law.

Sources checked:

- Kenya Law, [Law of Contract Act, Cap. 23](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1960/43/eng@2022-12-31), latest version shown by Kenya Law as at 31 December 2022. This supports the written contract, signature, incorporation by reference, and electronic-signature posture.
- Kenya Law, [Companies Act, 2015](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2015/17/eng%402023-09-15/source), especially execution of documents by companies. This shaped the signatory authority and company execution blocks.
- Kenya Law, [Data Protection Act, 2019](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng@2022-12-31) and [Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2021/263/eng@2022-12-31). These shaped the website data, Substack, access credentials, and processor-style obligations.
- Kenya Law, [Copyright Act, Cap. 130](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2001/12/eng@2022-12-31). This shaped the copyright, software ownership, licence, and assignment/recordal warning.
- Kenya Law, [Stamp Duty Act, Cap. 480](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1958/31/eng@2025-07-01), latest version shown by Kenya Law as at 1 July 2025. This supports a stamp-duty review note before signing.
- Kenya Revenue Authority, [Withholding Tax FAQ](https://www.kra.go.ke/helping-tax-payers/faqs/everything-about-withholding-tax). This supports including tax, withholding, and VAT allocation without stating unverified rates.

Key legal issues shaping this draft:

- Because Rotsi Labs will retain ownership of the codebase, the client should receive a clear production-use licence after payment instead of an ownership assignment.
- If the site collects names, emails, leads, analytics identifiers, or newsletter subscribers, data protection terms should be included.
- If either party signs as a company, the signing block should confirm authority and capacity.
- Fees should say whether VAT is included or excluded and how withholding tax, if applicable, is handled.
- Local counsel should confirm stamp duty, tax handling, electronic signing, and IP recordal issues before signing.

### 1. Definitions

In this Agreement:

**Acceptance Period** means **[5 business days]** after delivery of a milestone, preview link, staging site, production deployment, or other deliverable for review.

**Booking Data** means personal data submitted by prospective customers or guests through the Website for booking enquiries, including names, contact details, requested date or time, number of guests, message content, and any other booking fields agreed in Schedule 1.

**Change Request** means any requested change to scope, deliverables, timeline, technical assumptions, fees, design, user interface, functionality, integrations, content structure, hosting setup, or support obligations after agreement of the relevant scope or after final approval.

**Client Content** means all text, images, logos, trademarks, domain names, business information, brand materials, product information, contact details, newsletter content, and other materials supplied by or on behalf of the Client.

**Codebase** means the source code, scripts, components, layouts, configuration, templates, development environment, deployment scripts, reusable libraries, technical architecture, and repository used to create or maintain the Website.

**Final Approval** means the Client's written approval of the Website for launch or completion, including approval by email, signed approval form, project management comment, or other written record.

**Maintenance Services** means functional maintenance for the Website going down or being materially unavailable due to a fault within the Service Provider's agreed responsibility, as more specifically described in Schedule 2.

**Project** means the landing-page website design, website development, Substack integration, booking-related data collection setup, deployment support, and related services described in Schedule 1.

**Substack Integration** means the integration of Substack-related newsletter subscription, link, embed, routing, or other agreed Substack functionality described in Schedule 1, subject to Substack's own platform rules and availability.

**Website** means the website to be designed and developed for the Client under this Agreement.

2.1 The Client appoints the Service Provider to provide the Project services, and the Service Provider accepts the appointment, subject to this Agreement.

2.2 This Agreement starts on **18 May 2026** and continues until completion of the Project and expiry of the Maintenance Services, unless terminated earlier under this Agreement.

2.3 The Parties expect the Project to be completed within a maximum of **3 weeks** from the start date. 2.4 Any further design, development, UI modification, feature work, content updates, integration work, or support after the agreed scope or after Final Approval must be agreed through a separate written Change Request, statement of work, or contract.

- recovery from faults caused by the Client, third-party platforms, hosting providers, domain registrars, DNS providers, Substack, payment providers, or unauthorised changes by third parties.

6.1 The Client shall pay the Service Provider the fees set out in Schedule 3.

7.1 The Service Provider shall submit deliverables for review at the milestones stated in Schedule 1.

7.5 If the Client does not approve or validly reject a deliverable within the Acceptance Period, the deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

8.2 A Change Request may adjust fees, timelines, acceptance criteria, maintenance obligations, and third-party costs.

8.3 After Final Approval, any UI modification, redesign, layout change, feature request, new integration, content restructure, or enhancement is excluded from Maintenance Services and requires a separate written agreement, Change Request, or new contract.
